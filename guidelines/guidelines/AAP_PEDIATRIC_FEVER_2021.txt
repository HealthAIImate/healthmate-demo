DEFINITION OF FEVER IN CHILDREN:
Rectal temperature ≥ 38.0°C (100.4°F) — rectal is most accurate in infants
Oral temperature ≥ 37.5°C (99.5°F)
Axillary (armpit) temperature ≥ 37.2°C (99°F) — least accurate method
Tympanic (ear) temperature ≥ 37.5°C

AGE-BASED FEVER MANAGEMENT:

AGE < 3 MONTHS (< 90 days) — ALWAYS GO TO ER:
- ANY fever ≥ 38.0°C rectal = EMERGENCY — ER immediately, no exceptions
- Infants this age cannot mount full immune response
- Bacterial infections (sepsis, meningitis) can be rapidly life-threatening
- Do NOT give fever reducers before ER evaluation — masks important diagnostic information
- Do NOT wait to see if fever comes down — go immediately
- Do NOT call pediatrician first — go to ER directly

AGE 3-6 MONTHS with fever ≥ 38.0°C:
- Physician evaluation same day
- If well-appearing and vaccinated: pediatrician call first, may be evaluated in office
- If ill-appearing, lethargic, or difficult to console: ER

AGE 6 MONTHS - 2 YEARS with fever ≥ 39°C (102.2°F):
- If well-appearing and vaccinated: may manage at home with OTC
- If fever ≥ 40°C (104°F): physician evaluation same day
- If ill-appearing, not drinking, or inconsolable: physician evaluation

AGE > 2 YEARS with fever ≥ 39°C:
- May manage at home if well-appearing and drinking fluids
- Return to physician if fever persists > 3 days
- Physician same day if any of the red flags below

WEIGHT-BASED DOSING (AAP approved — ALWAYS use weight, not age alone):

Acetaminophen (Infant Tylenol 160mg/5mL concentration — ONLY use this concentration):
- Dose: 15mg/kg per dose (range 10-15mg/kg)
- Example: 6kg baby = 90mg = 2.8mL of 160mg/5mL
- Example: 10kg child = 150mg = 4.7mL of 160mg/5mL
- Frequency: every 4-6 hours as needed
- Maximum: 5 doses in 24 hours. Do not exceed.
- NEVER use adult Tylenol (higher concentration) for infants

Ibuprofen (Children's Motrin/Advil 100mg/5mL — for age ≥ 6 MONTHS ONLY):
- Dose: 10mg/kg per dose
- Example: 8kg baby = 80mg = 4mL of 100mg/5mL
- Example: 15kg child = 150mg = 7.5mL of 100mg/5mL
- Frequency: every 6-8 hours as needed
- Maximum: 4 doses in 24 hours
- NEVER give to infants under 6 months

NEVER:
- NEVER give aspirin to any child (Reye syndrome — fatal liver and brain damage)
- NEVER alternate Tylenol and Motrin unless specifically directed by physician (overdose risk)
- NEVER use adult formulations in children
- NEVER estimate dose by age — always use weight

FEVER RED FLAGS REQUIRING IMMEDIATE ER REGARDLESS OF TEMPERATURE:
- Inconsolable crying, cannot be comforted
- Bulging fontanelle (soft spot on top of head in infants)
- Stiff neck (cannot touch chin to chest)
- Petechial rash (small red/purple dots that don't fade with pressure — do the glass test)
- Difficulty breathing, fast breathing
- Lips or fingernails turning blue
- Seizure (even if first seizure in a child with fever)
- Confusion or altered consciousness
- Difficulty waking up
- Severe headache
- Age < 3 months with any fever
